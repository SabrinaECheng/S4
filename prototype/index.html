How Neura works

  1. Click any node to read its summary, the quotes behind it and what it connects to.

  2. Highlight a type from the Highlight key: questions, actions, quotes and more.

  3. Zoom in and every node turns into a card you can read.

  4. Drop a PDF, file or link anywhere and Neura files it under the idea it matches.

  5. Next steps turns the session into a checklist of small tasks.
